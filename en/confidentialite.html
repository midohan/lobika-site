Privacy

Lobika collects no data. Everything stays on your phone and your watch. Here is what the app does, and what it does not do.

Publisher: AGILE MOTION, SASU, RCS Lyon 901 934 521. This page applies to the Lobika app on iPhone, Apple Watch, Android and Wear OS. Last updated: 7 November 2026.

No data collected

Lobika has no account, no server, no advertising, no audience measurement tool and no crash reporting. AGILE MOTION receives nothing from the app: not your matches, not the first names of your crew, not an identifier. On Android, the app does not have permission to access the Internet.

What stays on your device

Players (a first name or a nickname, a preferred side), pairs, matches, tournaments and your settings are written to a file on your phone and, for whatever the watch has scored, on your watch. The app never asks for a player's sex and does not infer it. An installation identifier, created on first launch, is used to recognise your own devices among themselves; it is not an identifier of the device or of you.

System backups. On iPhone and Apple Watch, this file is backed up with the device, to iCloud or to your computer, if you have turned that backup on: it is an Apple service, under your account. On Android and Wear OS, the app is not included in the system backup.

Your friends' first names

The first names or nicknames you enter are those of people who play with you; you keep them for your own use. They leave your device only if you send a match (by QR code or by file), if you share a result card, or if you export your logbook: the app warns you first, and you can choose initials rather than first names on the card.

Between your phone and your watch

The iPhone and the Apple Watch exchange matches through Apple's channel between the two devices; the Android phone and the Wear OS watch, through Google's channel between the two devices. No AGILE MOTION server is involved.

Health and Health Connect: write only

If you allow it, each finished or stopped match is recorded as a session in Health (Apple) or Health Connect (Android): its start, its end, under the Tennis type, because these services have no padel type. Nothing else is written: no score, no first name, no heart rate, no energy. The app reads nothing from Health or Health Connect, and uses this writing for nothing else. Declining changes nothing about scoring. Sessions already written stay in Health or Health Connect, even after "Erase everything": you can delete them there.

The camera

The camera is used only to read the QR code of a match someone shows you, at the moment you ask for it. No image is saved or transmitted.

Purchases

Premium is taken as an annual subscription or a lifetime purchase, through the App Store or Google Play, under your Apple or Google account and their terms. AGILE MOTION sees neither your payment method nor your identity: Apple and Google send it aggregated sales reports (quantities, countries, amounts). The app keeps on your device the fact that Premium is active, without any payment token.

The review request

After your fifth finished match, the app may ask the system to offer you to leave a review, at most once per version. The window and what you write in it belong to the App Store or Google Play, not to Lobika.

Erasing

In Settings, "Erase everything" deletes players, pairs, matches and tournaments from the device, after confirmation. A file or a card already sent cannot be erased remotely. Deleting the app deletes its file.

Changes
