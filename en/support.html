Support

Answers to the most common questions, and how to write to us.

Write to us

Contact address: contact@agilemotion.fr. Tell us your device (iPhone, Apple Watch, Android phone, Wear OS watch), the app version (in Settings) and what you did just before. Do not send your logbook if you would rather not: it contains the first names of your crew.

Common questions

Does the watch score without the phone?

Yes. A full match is scored on the Apple Watch or on the Wear OS watch, with no phone nearby. The matches reach the phone afterwards, when the two meet again.

One tap too many?

Hold the central band of the screen until the ring fills: the last point is undone. You can undo as many points as needed, and even the last point of a finished match as long as you have not left the end screen.

Which game mode to choose?

The one your club or your crew plays: advantage, golden point, or two advantages then a deciding point. All three are described on the rules page (in Spanish). At the very first match, the app suggests advantage.

The match stopped at the end of the slot: is it lost?

No. Its score is frozen and it is marked "unfinished" in the history. To resume it, open it and undo the stop.

How do I give a match to a friend?

From the match detail, "Send" shows a QR code that their phone reads in Lobika, or a file to pass on to them. They match the players to their logbook. Sending and importing a match are free.

What does Premium include?

Pair chemistry and your crew's ranking, the organiser of Americano and Mexicano (pages in Spanish), advanced stats and logbook export. Scoring, game formats, history, basic stats, the share card and writing to Health or Health Connect stay free forever. The price is the one shown in the store; a subscription is managed and cancelled in the settings of your Apple or Google account.

Is my data sent anywhere?

No: see the privacy page.

Languages

The app comes in 14 languages and follows your device's language. Does a translation look wrong to you? Write to us: all of them were proofread carefully, but a phrasing may have slipped through, and we will fix it.
